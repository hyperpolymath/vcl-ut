When a statement genuinely has implementation-independent content,
port or reprove its exact theorem first in Agda:

[source,shell]
----
agda --safe --without-K --cubical-compatible <module.agda>
----

Then separately inspect the equivalent claim in Coq/Rocq and Lean.
A compiler success, matching theorem name, or translated type without
a statement correspondence proof is not a reconciled witness. Record
prover/library versions, source revisions, stated assumptions and
the correspondence receipt in the owning proof status.

The canonical cross-estate policy lives in
`metadatastician/standards/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`.
Mathesis is an index, and ECHIDNA is a future automation route,
not dependencies of VCL-UT's own soundness.
