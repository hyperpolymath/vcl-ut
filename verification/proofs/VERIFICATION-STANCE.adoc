WARNING: vcl-ut is the interface between *hypatia* and *verisim*; both sides
trust safety claims made here. This document states *precisely* what is and is
not machine-verified, and takes precedence over any "verified" / "type-safe" /
"formally proven" phrasing in `README.adoc`, `PROOF-NEEDS.md`, or module
doc-comments. It is the proof-backed companion to (and supersedes) the
doc-only `docs/VERIFICATION-STANCE.adoc` (PR #19).

Tracked under hyperpolymath/standards#124.

== Headline finding (Phase 0 audit) — and Phase 1 + 2 status

*Phase 0 audit (origin/main, reproduced with idris2 0.8.0):* the shipped
Idris2 "proof corpus" *did not compile at all*, at every layer, and had
*never been machine-checked* (no `.ipkg`/Makefile/CI for `src/core/**` —
only the unrelated trivial `src/abi/vclut.ipkg`, itself absent from CI).
The previously-circulated stance ("the dependent-type apparatus exists but
is not connected") was *too generous*: the apparatus did not even
typecheck. The concrete blockers were:

* `src/interface/abi/Types.idr` — missing `import Decidable.Equality`;
  `DecEq SafetyLevel/VclTotalError/QueryMode` malformed
  (`decEq _ _ = No absurd`, no `Uninhabited`); `CPtr p _ = Bits (ptrSize
  p)` ill-typed; `createQueryHandle` omitted the `So (ptr /= 0)` witness;
  `thisPlatform` used `%runElab` without `%language ElabReflection`.
* `src/core/Grammar.idr` — `Expr`/`Statement`/`EpistemicClause`/
  `EpistemicRequirement` mutually forward-referenced with no `mutual`
  block.
* Latent (never compiled): `Schema` (name-shadowing in `lookupField`),
  `Levels` (helper predicates declared after the level data they index),
  `Checker` (mis-scoped `where` for `agentEq`, `CheckResult` ambiguity,
  `let`-tuple parse, and a non-structural `extractFieldRefs ⇄
  statementFieldRefs` recursion across `ESubquery Statement`),
  `Composition` (forward refs, deleted `AllParameterised` still
  referenced, wrong `CertL6+` arities, non-reducing `rewrite`s).

*Phase 1 + 2 + 3 status (RESOLVED):* every blocker above is fixed
*honestly* and the corpus compiles. **Phase 2** de-vacuized L2/L3/L5
(new shared decider module `VclTotal.Core.Decide`, evidence-carrying
predicates, `checkLevel2/3/5Sound`, genuine `composeJoin` closure).
**Phase 3** added soundness for L1 and L6–L10
(`checkLevel1Sound`…`checkLevel10Sound`), a genuine dependent
`SafetyCertificate` emitter (`Checker.certifyAt`/`certifyRequested`),
and made `VclTotal.ABI.Layout` sound + CI-gated (unsound claims
deleted, not faked). `idris2 0.8.0 --build
verification/proofs/vclut-core.ipkg` exits 0 over `VclTotal.ABI.{Types,
Layout}` + `VclTotal.Core.{Grammar,Schema,Decide,Levels,Checker,
Composition,Epistemic}` (9 modules), under `%default total`,
with *zero*
`believe_me`/`really_believe_me`/`postulate`/`assert_total`/
`assert_smaller`/`idris_crash`/`sorry` (CI-enforced by
`.github/workflows/proof-corpus.yml`, which also scans for proof-escape
symbols and rebuilds `SafetyL4Model.idr`). Notable honest techniques:
real `DecEq` via an injective C-tag round-trip + `DecEq Bits32`;
`Checker`'s `extractFieldRefs/statementFieldRefs` made structurally total
by pattern-matching `MkStatement` (no fuel/axiom/`assert_smaller`);
`Composition` re-derived with a genuine list-membership L1 subset proof, a
genuine L4 (`noRawUserInputCompose`), genuine L6–L10 equational proofs,
and a fully-qualified `hasStringLit` (a bare lowercase occurrence in a
*type* signature is silently auto-bound by Idris 2 as a fresh implicit,
which had decoupled the predicate from its definition).

*New finding (Phase 1):* `src/interface/abi/Layout.idr` and
`src/interface/abi/Foreign.idr` (`VclTotal.ABI.Layout/.Foreign`) do *not*
compile under idris2 0.8.0 and contain *unsound* scaffolding (`paddingFor`
uses `(-)` on `Nat`; `alignUpCorrect` asserts `n = (n \`div\` a) * a` by
`Refl`; `queryPlanHeaderNoPadding` asserts a false size equality). They
are outside Phase 1's enumerated scope (ABI.Types + Core.*), nothing in
`Core.*` imports them, and they are deliberately excluded from
`vclut-core.ipkg`. See OWED below.

== What IS genuinely machine-verified by this PR

`verification/proofs/SafetyL4Model.idr` — checked by **idris2 0.8.0,
`idris2 --check`, exit 0, with `%default total` and ZERO
`believe_me`/`really_believe_me`/`postulate`/`assert_total`/`idris_crash`/
`sorry`**. It reproduces the *exact mathematical content* of the in-repo
Level-4 (SQL-injection) remediation against a minimal, self-contained,
byte-faithful model of the relevant AST fragment, proving:

. `NoRawUserInput` is no longer *vacuous*. It previously had the single
  catch-all constructor `AllParameterised : NoRawUserInput stmt`, inhabiting
  it for *every* statement — including a WHERE clause that is pure string
  interpolation. It now carries real evidence: `whereHasStringLit w = False`.
. `checkLevel4Sound` — *soundness of the decision procedure*: if
  `checkLevel4` accepts, the statement genuinely carries the (now real)
  `NoRawUserInput` witness. This is the certificate↔checker connection for
  L4; it was previously unstateable (predicate vacuous, checker returned a
  bare `Bool`).
. `injectionRejected` — negative test: a WHERE bearing a string literal
  (`… = '1=1'`) is *rejected*. Under the old vacuous predicate the
  corresponding query "type-checked" at Level 4.
. `noRawUserInputCompose` — Level-4 injection-freedom is *genuinely* closed
  under join composition, because `joinWhere` introduces no string literal
  not already present. This replaces the two
  `MkL4 _ AllParameterised` uses in `Composition.compositionPreservation`,
  whose "preservation theorem" was therefore *vacuously true*.

As of Phase 1 the in-repo source fix is no longer model-only: it is
machine-checked *in situ*. `Checker.checkLevel4Sound`
(`checkLevel4 stmt = (True, m) -> L4_InjectionProof stmt`, proved with
`with (whereHasStringLit stmt) proof p`, no escape) and
`Composition.noRawUserInputCompose` typecheck inside the compiling corpus
and are *identical in content* to the `SafetyL4Model.idr` lemmas of the
same names (model: `MkNoRawUserInput : whereHasStringLit w = False ->
NoRawUserInput w`; in-repo: `L4_InjectionProof = MkL4 stmt (NoRawUserInput
stmt)` with the same `whereHasStringLit stmt = False` evidence). L4
injection-freedom is therefore now genuine *and* wired through the
type-checked corpus — though still not through the (stubbed) FFI.

*Phase 2* extends the same architecture to L2/L3/L5 *in the corpus*
(there is no separate model file for these — they are verified in situ
by the CI `--build`): predicates carry a `= True` equality on the
shared `VclTotal.Core.Decide` deciders; `Checker.checkLevel2/3/5` are
defined through those deciders; `checkLevel2Sound`/`checkLevel3Sound`/
`checkLevel5Sound` prove acceptance ⇒ predicate (same `with … proof p`,
no escape, as L4); and `Composition`'s `l2Compose`/`l3Compose`/
`l5Compose` are genuine (L3 via guard-set monotonicity). The remaining
honest gaps (uncertified `checkQuery`; L1/L6–L10 soundness; L3 subquery/
heuristic scoping; unsound excluded `ABI.Layout/.Foreign`) are in the
OWED section.

== What is NOT proven (honest, precisely-scoped gaps — OWED)

. *(Phase 1 — RESOLVED)* The Phase-1 corpus (ABI.Types + Core.*) now
  compiles and is CI-gated; in-repo L4 is machine-checked in situ. No
  longer outstanding.
. *(Phase 3c — RESOLVED for `ABI.Layout`; re-scoped for `ABI.Foreign`).*
  `VclTotal.ABI.Layout` is now *sound, compiling, and CI-gated* (it is
  the 9th module in `vclut-core.ipkg`). The three unsound claims were
  *deleted, not faked*: `alignUpCorrect` (used `(align > 0)`, a `Bool`,
  as a type, and a bogus `Refl` asserting the divisibility goal);
  `offsetInBounds` (an open `?metavariable` — never a proof, false in
  general); and `queryPlanHeaderNoPadding` (over-claimed "no padding",
  and even the trivial `totalSize = 24` restatement does not reduce
  under `Refl` because `StructLayout.fields : Vect n Field` blocks the
  projection). `paddingFor`'s genuine non-compile (`(-)` needs `Neg
  Nat`) was *fixed* to `minus`. The sound content (the
  `safetyLevel`/`queryMode`/`vqlUtError` round-trip `Refl` proofs, the
  layout data, `Divides`/`alignUp`/`paddingFor`) remains and is now
  machine-checked. *Carried OWED (Phase 3, precisely):* the genuine
  alignment/no-padding theorems (`alignUp` divisibility via real
  `div`/`mod` lemmas; fold field sizes = `totalSize`) and a
  membership-quantified `offsetInBounds`.
  `VclTotal.ABI.Foreign` is *deliberately still excluded* but is *not
  unsound*: it contains **no proofs at all** — only `%foreign` C
  declarations and `Maybe`/`Either` wrappers. Its gaps are (a) it does
  not compile as-is (FFI plumbing: missing `Data.Bits`, etc.) and (b)
  the architectural stub-FFI issue (the level tag is an *asserted
  integer* from the Zig side, not a transported proof). That is genuine
  Phase 3+ architecture (implement the Zig/Rust side; transport an
  opaque proof token), OWED and disclosed — not a false proof.
. *(Phase 2 — RESOLVED) L2/L3/L5 predicates de-vacuized.* The
  content-free constructors (`ExprTypeSafe`/`WhereTypeSafe`,
  `AllNullableFieldsGuarded`'s `GuardedNull`, `AllSelectItemsTyped`'s
  `ConsTyped`) are *deleted*. Each predicate now carries a `= True`
  equality on a shared decider in the new module `VclTotal.Core.Decide`
  (the single source of truth, the same architecture as
  `Grammar.hasStringLit` for L4): `whereComparisonsCompatible` (L2 —
  every `ECompare` in WHERE has compatible resolved operand types),
  `nullSafeStmt` (L3 — neither WHERE nor HAVING uses a schema-nullable
  field without an explicit NULL guard; L3 is now *Statement*-indexed so
  it actually covers what the checker checks), `selectItemsTyped` (L5 —
  every SELECT item resolves to a non-`TAny` type). `Checker.checkLevel2
  /3/5` are *defined through* those same functions, and
  `checkLevel2Sound` / `checkLevel3Sound` / `checkLevel5Sound` prove
  acceptance ⇒ predicate (mirroring `checkLevel4Sound`). *Disclosed
  scoping* (sound, but bounding what the claim means): L3's
  `exprFieldRefsD` / `nullGuardedRefs` treat `ESubquery` as opaque (a
  subquery's null-safety is its own pass's concern, not folded into the
  enclosing query); the L3 NULL-guard recogniser is the original
  *structural* heuristic (`fld = NULL` / `NULL = fld` / `fld <> NULL` /
  `NULL <> fld` through the boolean structure), not a dataflow analysis;
  L2/L5 type resolution is the existing annotation/schema lookup, no
  inference. These are the properties the checker *and* the proof now
  jointly mean — no stronger, no weaker.
. *(Phase 3a/3b — RESOLVED in-corpus) The decision procedure now emits a
  genuine dependent `SafetyCertificate`.* All ten levels have soundness
  lemmas — `checkLevel1Sound` … `checkLevel10Sound` (L1's
  `Decide.allFieldsBoundFromResolve` *builds* the inductive
  `AllFieldsBound`, each `FieldBound` carrying the real `resolveFieldRef
  ref schema = Just fd`). `Checker.certifyAt` / `certifyRequested`
  assemble them: a `Just` result *is* a machine-checked
  `SafetyCertificate stmt schema (requestedLevel stmt)`. `checkQuery`
  (the `Bool`/`CheckResult` C-ABI path) is retained unchanged.
  *Carried OWED (Phase 3, precisely):* the L6–L10 *predicates* assert
  only "the relevant clause/annotation is present" — real and
  non-vacuous, but shallower than what `checkLevelN` additionally
  enforces (L9 ≠ `LinUnlimited`; L10 declared agents + acyclic
  `ENTAILS`); strengthening those predicates is future work. And the
  certificate is still *not transported across the FFI* (see the
  `ABI.Foreign` item) — the in-corpus certificate is not yet a runtime
  proof token handed to hypatia/verisim.
. *`Composition.compositionPreservation`* (compiles): L0/L1/L2/L3/L4/L5/
  L6–L10 are now *all genuine*. L1 = a real list-membership subset proof
  (`composeJoinFieldsSubset`); L4 = `noRawUserInputCompose`; L6–L10 =
  equational proofs about the `join*` combiners; **L2** =
  `whereCompatJoin` (`extractComparisons` distributes over the conjoined
  `ELogic` node); **L5** = `selTypedAppend` (per-item, list-append);
  **L3** = `nullSafeStmtCompose` via guard-set *monotonicity*
  (`refUnguardedWeaken{L,R}` — a larger guard set only un-flags refs)
  plus list-append, with HAVING dropped by `composeJoin` (trivially
  null-safe). No `lN Compose` relies on a vacuous constructor any more.
. *(Phase 3d — lie removed; FFI is now fail-closed, the boundary
  precisely scoped).* `ffi/zig/src/lib.zig` previously returned `2`
  (`Verified L2`) for *any* query containing the substring `SELECT` — a
  fabricated safety level. That has been **deleted**: the shim is now
  *fail-closed* (`vclut_verify_query` returns `-1` Rejected with an
  explicit "no verifier backend linked" message; it never returns a
  level it did not establish), the Zig test that enshrined the lie was
  rewritten to assert the fail-closed contract, and `zig build test`
  passes. A genuine *proof-gated attestation mint* now exists in the
  CI-gated corpus: `Checker.certifiedLevel : Statement -> OctadSchema ->
  Int` returns a level *only* on the `Just`-certificate branch of
  `certifyRequested` (structurally — no proof-escape).
+
  *Inherent boundary (not a defect).* A C ABI cannot transport a
  dependent `SafetyCertificate`; the honest model is *trusted-certifier
  attestation* (as in proof-carrying code: the consumer trusts the
  checker that minted the integer). What a hypatia/verisim caller
  receives is therefore at most an attestation from the Idris certifier,
  never a re-checkable proof object — by construction of any FFI.
+
  *NAMED OWED (absent, not faked):* (1) **no string→`Statement`
  parser exists anywhere in the repo** — the corpus certifies an
  already-built AST, so the `verify(query_string)` FFI shape
  *presupposes a component nobody has written*; (2) C-ABI
  `Statement`/`OctadSchema` marshalling; (3) the Idris→C build that
  would let the shim actually call `certifiedLevel`; (4) the real
  backend symbol (`vclut_rs_verify`) is declared in intent but not
  linked. Until (1)–(4) exist the FFI is honestly fail-closed.

== Consumer guidance (hypatia / verisim)

* Treat *every* vcl-ut safety-level tag as an *advisory claim*, not a proof
  carrier. Do not weaken your own checks on the strength of a vcl-ut level.
* As of Phase 3, *all ten levels* (L1–L10) have a genuine
  machine-checked basis in the compiling, CI-gated corpus: non-vacuous
  predicates, deciders the checker is defined through, `checkLevelNSound`
  lemmas, and (L1–L10) closure under `composeJoin`; and
  `Checker.certifyAt`/`certifyRequested` assemble a genuine dependent
  `SafetyCertificate`. **However** this certificate is *not transported
  across the FFI* (the Zig/Rust side is a stub; the level tag a
  hypatia/verisim caller receives is an asserted integer, not the proof
  token). Cite vcl-ut at most as "machine-checked L1–L10 safety
  predicates + per-level checker soundness + a dependent
  `SafetyCertificate`, for the in-repo AST model, *not transported
  across the FFI*", noting the disclosed L3 subquery/heuristic scoping
  and the L6–L10 predicate shallowness above. Do not weaken your own
  checks on the strength of a vcl-ut FFI level tag.
* `VclTotal.ABI.Layout` is now *sound + CI-gated* (unsound claims
  deleted, not faked); its genuine alignment/no-padding theorems are
  OWED. `VclTotal.ABI.Foreign` contains *no proofs* (FFI plumbing) — do
  not treat its returned level tag as a verified claim.

  *Residual (carried):* `checkQuery` still does not emit a

== Provenance

Phase 0 audit + remediation 2026-05-18 (hyperpolymath/standards#124),
findings reproduced by `idris2 0.8.0 --build`/`--check`. Phase 1
resurrection 2026-05-18: corpus compiles clean. Phase 2 2026-05-19:
L2/L3/L5 de-vacuized (new `VclTotal.Core.Decide`). Phase 3 2026-05-19:
L1/L6–L10 soundness + `certifyAt`/`certifyRequested` dependent
certificate + `ABI.Layout` made sound & gated; Phase 3d 2026-05-19:
FFI fabricated-level lie deleted (fail-closed, `zig build test` green) +
proof-gated `Checker.certifiedLevel` mint. The corpus builds clean as
*9 modules*
(`idris2 0.8.0 --build verification/proofs/vclut-core.ipkg`, exit 0,
`%default total`, zero proof-escape symbols — CI-enforced), the added
modules being `VclTotal.Core.Decide` (shared deciders) and
`VclTotal.ABI.Layout` (now sound).
No proof-escape symbol was used to force any green; every obligation
that could not be honestly discharged is scoped OWED above rather than
masked.

Notes for reproduction / honesty:

* `Data.List.elemBy` in idris2 0.8.0 is a `foldl (… Delay …)` and does
  *not* reduce structurally, so the L3 guard membership uses a
  purpose-built `Decide.refElem` (plain `||` recursion). This is a
  proof-engineering choice, not a soundness gap.
* The `verification/proofs/corpus/` entries are git-mode symlinks
  (`120000`). On a worktree with `core.symlinks=false` they check out as
  path-text files; a build then either fails to parse them or, worse,
  *passes from stale `build/ttc`* — a false green. Always
  `rm -rf verification/proofs/build` and ensure the corpus entries are
  real symlinks before trusting a local result. CI (Linux,
  `core.symlinks` default true) is unaffected; this caveat is for local
  reproduction only.

The residual risk is now purely at the *boundary*, and the boundary no
longer lies: the FFI is *fail-closed* (no fabricated level; `zig build
test` green) and a genuine proof-gated mint (`Checker.certifiedLevel`)
exists in the CI-gated corpus. What remains OWED is *plumbing and an
unwritten parser*, not a soundness gap: there is **no string→`Statement`
parser anywhere in the repo** (so the `verify(query_string)` FFI shape
presupposes a component nobody has written), no C-ABI `Statement`
marshalling, and no Idris→C build linking the shim to the certifier;
plus the L6–L10 *predicate* shallowness, the genuine `ABI.Layout`
alignment/no-padding theorems, `ABI.Foreign` plumbing, and L3's
disclosed subquery/heuristic scoping. A C ABI carrying a *re-checkable
dependent proof* is impossible by construction — the honest target is
trusted-certifier attestation, and that trust boundary is stated, not
hidden. The former headline residuals (*non-compiling corpus*, *vacuous
L2/L3/L5 predicates*, *decorative certificate*, *unsound `ABI.Layout`*,
*lying FFI*) are all resolved.
