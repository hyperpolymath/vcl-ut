WARNING: vcl-ut is the interface between *hypatia* and *verisim*; both sides
trust safety claims made here. This document states *precisely* what is and is
not machine-verified, and takes precedence over any "verified" / "type-safe" /
"formally proven" phrasing in `README.adoc`, `PROOF-NEEDS.md`, or module
doc-comments. It is the proof-backed companion to (and supersedes) the
doc-only `docs/VERIFICATION-STANCE.adoc` (PR #19).

Tracked under hyperpolymath/standards#124.

== Headline finding (Phase 0 audit) — and Phase 1 status

*Phase 0 audit (origin/main, reproduced with idris2 0.8.0):* the shipped
Idris2 "proof corpus" *did not compile at all*, at every layer, and had
*never been machine-checked* (no `.ipkg`/Makefile/CI for `src/core/**` —
only the unrelated trivial `src/abi/vclut.ipkg`, itself absent from CI).
The previously-circulated stance ("the dependent-type apparatus exists but
is not connected") was *too generous*: the apparatus did not even
typecheck. The concrete blockers were:

* `src/interface/abi/Types.idr` — missing `import Decidable.Equality`;
  `DecEq SafetyLevel/VclTotalError/QueryMode` malformed
  (`decEq _ _ = No absurd`, no `Uninhabited`); `CPtr p _ = Bits (ptrSize
  p)` ill-typed; `createQueryHandle` omitted the `So (ptr /= 0)` witness;
  `thisPlatform` used `%runElab` without `%language ElabReflection`.
* `src/core/Grammar.idr` — `Expr`/`Statement`/`EpistemicClause`/
  `EpistemicRequirement` mutually forward-referenced with no `mutual`
  block.
* Latent (never compiled): `Schema` (name-shadowing in `lookupField`),
  `Levels` (helper predicates declared after the level data they index),
  `Checker` (mis-scoped `where` for `agentEq`, `CheckResult` ambiguity,
  `let`-tuple parse, and a non-structural `extractFieldRefs ⇄
  statementFieldRefs` recursion across `ESubquery Statement`),
  `Composition` (forward refs, deleted `AllParameterised` still
  referenced, wrong `CertL6+` arities, non-reducing `rewrite`s).

*Phase 1 status (this PR — RESOLVED):* every blocker above is fixed
*honestly* and the Phase-1 corpus now compiles. `idris2 0.8.0 --build
verification/proofs/vclut-core.ipkg` exits 0 over
`VclTotal.ABI.Types` + `VclTotal.Core.{Grammar,Schema,Levels,Checker,
Composition,Epistemic}` (7 modules), under `%default total`, with *zero*
`believe_me`/`really_believe_me`/`postulate`/`assert_total`/
`assert_smaller`/`idris_crash`/`sorry` (CI-enforced by
`.github/workflows/proof-corpus.yml`, which also scans for proof-escape
symbols and rebuilds `SafetyL4Model.idr`). Notable honest techniques:
real `DecEq` via an injective C-tag round-trip + `DecEq Bits32`;
`Checker`'s `extractFieldRefs/statementFieldRefs` made structurally total
by pattern-matching `MkStatement` (no fuel/axiom/`assert_smaller`);
`Composition` re-derived with a genuine list-membership L1 subset proof, a
genuine L4 (`noRawUserInputCompose`), genuine L6–L10 equational proofs,
and a fully-qualified `hasStringLit` (a bare lowercase occurrence in a
*type* signature is silently auto-bound by Idris 2 as a fresh implicit,
which had decoupled the predicate from its definition).

*New finding (Phase 1):* `src/interface/abi/Layout.idr` and
`src/interface/abi/Foreign.idr` (`VclTotal.ABI.Layout/.Foreign`) do *not*
compile under idris2 0.8.0 and contain *unsound* scaffolding (`paddingFor`
uses `(-)` on `Nat`; `alignUpCorrect` asserts `n = (n \`div\` a) * a` by
`Refl`; `queryPlanHeaderNoPadding` asserts a false size equality). They
are outside Phase 1's enumerated scope (ABI.Types + Core.*), nothing in
`Core.*` imports them, and they are deliberately excluded from
`vclut-core.ipkg`. See OWED below.

== What IS genuinely machine-verified by this PR

`verification/proofs/SafetyL4Model.idr` — checked by **idris2 0.8.0,
`idris2 --check`, exit 0, with `%default total` and ZERO
`believe_me`/`really_believe_me`/`postulate`/`assert_total`/`idris_crash`/
`sorry`**. It reproduces the *exact mathematical content* of the in-repo
Level-4 (SQL-injection) remediation against a minimal, self-contained,
byte-faithful model of the relevant AST fragment, proving:

. `NoRawUserInput` is no longer *vacuous*. It previously had the single
  catch-all constructor `AllParameterised : NoRawUserInput stmt`, inhabiting
  it for *every* statement — including a WHERE clause that is pure string
  interpolation. It now carries real evidence: `whereHasStringLit w = False`.
. `checkLevel4Sound` — *soundness of the decision procedure*: if
  `checkLevel4` accepts, the statement genuinely carries the (now real)
  `NoRawUserInput` witness. This is the certificate↔checker connection for
  L4; it was previously unstateable (predicate vacuous, checker returned a
  bare `Bool`).
. `injectionRejected` — negative test: a WHERE bearing a string literal
  (`… = '1=1'`) is *rejected*. Under the old vacuous predicate the
  corresponding query "type-checked" at Level 4.
. `noRawUserInputCompose` — Level-4 injection-freedom is *genuinely* closed
  under join composition, because `joinWhere` introduces no string literal
  not already present. This replaces the two
  `MkL4 _ AllParameterised` uses in `Composition.compositionPreservation`,
  whose "preservation theorem" was therefore *vacuously true*.

As of Phase 1 the in-repo source fix is no longer model-only: it is
machine-checked *in situ*. `Checker.checkLevel4Sound`
(`checkLevel4 stmt = (True, m) -> L4_InjectionProof stmt`, proved with
`with (whereHasStringLit stmt) proof p`, no escape) and
`Composition.noRawUserInputCompose` typecheck inside the compiling corpus
and are *identical in content* to the `SafetyL4Model.idr` lemmas of the
same names (model: `MkNoRawUserInput : whereHasStringLit w = False ->
NoRawUserInput w`; in-repo: `L4_InjectionProof = MkL4 stmt (NoRawUserInput
stmt)` with the same `whereHasStringLit stmt = False` evidence). L4
injection-freedom is therefore now genuine *and* wired through the
type-checked corpus — though still not through the (stubbed) FFI.

== What is NOT proven (honest, precisely-scoped gaps — OWED)

. *(Phase 1 — RESOLVED)* The Phase-1 corpus (ABI.Types + Core.*) now
  compiles and is CI-gated; in-repo L4 is machine-checked in situ. No
  longer outstanding.
. *(Phase 1 — NEW OWED) `VclTotal.ABI.Layout` / `VclTotal.ABI.Foreign`
  do not compile and are unsound.* `paddingFor` applies `(-)` to `Nat`
  (no `Neg Nat`); `alignUpCorrect : … -> Divides align (alignUp size
  align)` is "proved" by `DivideBy (… \`div\` align) Refl`, asserting the
  false `alignUp size align = (alignUp size align \`div\` align) * align`;
  `queryPlanHeaderNoPadding` asserts `24 = queryPlanHeaderLayout.totalSize`
  which does not hold; `(align > 0)` (a `Bool`) is used as a type. These
  are *unsound layout claims*, not mere non-compilation. Out of Phase 1's
  enumerated scope (ABI.Types + Core.*); `Core.*` does not import them;
  excluded from `vclut-core.ipkg`. Must be repaired or deleted before any
  ABI memory-layout claim is relied upon (Phase 3 / FFI territory).
. *L2/L3/L5 predicates remain vacuous.* `AllComparisonsTypeSafe` via
  `WhereTypeSafe LogicSafe` (holds for any logic expr); `L3`
  `AllNullableFieldsGuarded`'s `GuardedNull` (accepts any expr); `L5`
  `AllSelectItemsTyped`'s `ConsTyped` (no per-item typing evidence). As
  written, Levels 2, 3 and 5 prove nothing about the property they name.
  Only L4 has been de-vacuized.
. *The certificate is disconnected from the checker for every level except
  the L4 evidence.* `Checker.checkQuery` returns a plain `CheckResult`
  record and never constructs a `SafetyCertificate`. The dependent types
  are decorative w.r.t. the decision procedure (L4 excepted, via
  `checkLevel4Sound`).
. *`Composition.compositionPreservation`* (now compiles): L0/L1/L4/L6–L10
  are *genuine* (L1 = a real list-membership subset proof
  `composeJoinFieldsSubset`; L4 = `noRawUserInputCompose`; L6–L10 =
  equational proofs about the `join*` combiners). The L2/L3/L5 cases are
  constructed at the correct *indexed* type but stay evidentially weak
  because the L2/L3/L5 *predicates* are still vacuous (next item);
  `Composition` self-documents this.
. *The FFI boundary is a stub.* `ffi/zig/src/lib.zig` returns `2`
  (`L2`) for any query containing the substring `SELECT`; the Rust
  `extern "C"` counterpart does not exist. `src/abi/Foreign.idr`
  fabricates `Verified Ln` from a bare untrusted `Int`. The level tag
  hypatia/verisim receive is an *asserted integer from a substring check*,
  not a transported proof.

== Consumer guidance (hypatia / verisim)

* Treat *every* vcl-ut safety-level tag as an *advisory claim*, not a proof
  carrier. Do not weaken your own checks on the strength of a vcl-ut level.
* The *only* claim with a genuine machine-checked basis is L4
  injection-freedom. As of Phase 1 it *is* wired through the compiling,
  CI-gated corpus (in situ `checkLevel4Sound` / `noRawUserInputCompose`) —
  but it is still *not* transported through the FFI (stubbed), L1 schema
  binding is genuine only as a corpus proof (not a runtime carrier), and
  L2/L3/L5 remain vacuous. Do not cite vcl-ut as "formally verified query
  safety" downstream; cite at most "machine-checked L4 injection-freedom
  of the in-repo AST model, not transported across the FFI".
* `VclTotal.ABI.Layout`/`.Foreign` carry *unsound* layout proofs — do not
  rely on any vcl-ut struct-layout/alignment claim.

== Remediation roadmap (phased, tracked standards#124)

. *Phase 0 (DONE):* de-vacuize L4 + checker soundness + real
  composition; machine-verify the content (`SafetyL4Model.idr`); document
  the true state.
. *Phase 1 (DONE, this PR):* corpus compiles — `mutual` in `Grammar.idr`;
  `ABI.Types` repaired (real `DecEq`, `CPtr`, `createQueryHandle`,
  `thisPlatform`); `Schema/Levels/Checker/Composition/Epistemic` latent
  errors fixed honestly (incl. structural totality for
  `extractFieldRefs`); `verification/proofs/vclut-core.ipkg` +
  `.github/workflows/proof-corpus.yml` so it stays compiled and
  escape-free; in-repo L4 verified in situ. *Residual:* `ABI.Layout/
  .Foreign` unsound + non-compiling (OWED, scoped to a follow-up).
. *Phase 2 (TODO):* de-vacuize L2/L3/L5 with evidence-carrying
  constructors and prove `checkLevelN` sound for each (as done for L4).
. *Phase 3 (TODO):* have `checkQuery` emit a real `SafetyCertificate`;
  transport it as an opaque proof token through a non-stub Zig/Rust FFI;
  repair or delete `ABI.Layout`/`.Foreign`.
