= Research direction: types, tropes and identity across spaces
:toc:
:sectnums:

Status: EXPLORATORY RESEARCH CONTEXT / reconnaissance, 2026-10-29.
This document does not modify the normative L0–L10 safety levels,
the source grammar, or VCLW/VCLS/VCLT wire contracts. It explains
questions motivating several independent language/database experiments,
not a selected architecture or ordered implementation mandate.
VCL-UT remains its own language/admission research system, not a
Hypatia-specific frontend. No type–trope equivalence or general
triangulation theorem is claimed. Its proposed judgements and work items
are candidates, not architectural decisions.

== Core question

A subject can have representations, occupy roles, hold capabilities,
instantiate properties, participate in protocols, persist through time
and be described by different witnesses. These are not one kind of
identity claim. Their legitimate operations, equivalence criteria,
evidence and proof obligations can differ. The relevant research
question is: **when may a proposition or transition cross between
those spaces without conflating distinct identities or inventing
authority, evidence or preserved particularity?**

Do not equate:

* equality of values with numerical identity of particulars;
* equality of type inhabitants with identity of bearers;
* structural compatibility with authority to occupy a role;
* membership of a protocol state with permission to act;
* identical observations with identical origins;
* two compatible witnesses with causally independent witnesses;
* retrieval, interpretability or loss-relative licensing with real-world truth;
* two presentations meeting a sortal criterion with an unconditional,
  global equivalence relation.

Type formalisms *can* encode individual records and intensional
distinctions; tropes *can* have rigorous operations and checkers.
The distinction is about what a judgement asserts and its evidence,
not that one side is mathematics and the other is metaphor.

== The motivating problem, not a predetermined solution

The owner is exploring **identity and equivalence**, including *synchronic*
questions (what agrees or counts as the same at a given observational
cut?) and *diachronic* questions (what survives change of
representation, algorithm, record, history and authority over time?).
Different systems are intentionally different probes of this question.
Nothing requires one global identity type, common coercion language,
a total hierarchy of kinds of space or a grand unified calculus.

Crucially, *equivalence under a chosen relation* is not automatically
*identity through a history*. A static equivalence relation can
identify two presentations at one boundary, while a transition
requires a directed, evidence-bearing account of what persisted,
what was intentionally changed, which commitments survived and who
had authority at each point. Conversely, a continuing service may
change its future decisions without breaking a narrower contract
of identity. Proof of either judgement must name its criteria.

The distinctions offer **tests of any eventual theory**, not inputs that
must all be incorporated into VCL-UT. Some useful questions are:

* Can two different presentations be equivalent under a named
  observer/sortal yet retain separate provenance or numerical identities?
* Can a single subject persist over time even when its representation,
  behaviour, effective authority or attached claims change? Which parts
  of its history are non-negotiable?
* If account A was warranted at t1 and later retracted at t2, what is
  the identity of the *account*, the *claim* and the *evidence*? How
  are event time, admission time and knowledge-revision time separated?
* When a path is missing, do we mean not-equivalent, no proof found,
  insufficient evidence, forbidden transformation or the relation is
  not even defined? These are materially different outcomes.
* Under what circumstances can multiple views count as independent
  corroboration, rather than duplicated presentations of one witness?

These questions also connect back to the independent type disciplines:
linear/affine/immutable/phantom types can maintain tokens or versions;
epistemic and residual-evidence types can expose what a view warrants;
trope grades can report alteration of situated particularity; and
session/choreographic/dyadic types can constrain transitions. None
of those mechanisms, individually, settles the general identity question.

|===

In particular, `occupancy-types` currently concerns resource/high-water-mark
occupancy. It should not be silently treated as a proof of legitimacy
for occupants of institutional positions.

A possible *research* judgement (notation illustrative, NOT an existing
VCL-UT syntax or new type constructor) is:

----
Γ ; sortal S ; context C ; time t ; evidence E
  ⊢ representation r : T
  ⊢ particular p borne-by b in C
  ⊢ bridge(r,p) : admissible under obligation O and warrant E
----

The bridge is a *partial and potentially many-to-many, direction-sensitive
relation*, not an assumed coercion or equivalence. A concrete bridge
must say exactly what it preserves and what it forgets, and name the
sortal and observer when identity claims are involved. A sortal may
supply criteria of identity and counting without being identical to
a type or a trope.

No claim is made that this is an exhaustive type taxonomy. Additional
candidates include higher-kinded, row, intersection, union, relevant,
ordered, bunched, refinement, Hoare, sized, gradual, cubical,
modal, deontic and observational-equality formalisms. Entries
must be classified by *mechanism*, *judgement*, *space*, *proof*, and
*operational enforcement*, not pushed into an invented L11+ ordering.

== The trope programme is already formal, not decorative

`trope-checker` has a typed IR and graded operation calculus with
Idris2 reference checking; its Agda `TB.Honest` development shows that
the specified honest constructors/composition do not generate
deceptive grades. `Haec` records separate lowering-correctness
obligations (O2). The October 2026 stack status identifies the
workbench as vocabulary/specification, Haec as design, and Vocarium
and Hermeneia as partial executable slices. Thus a theorem about
the grade algebra is **not** a theorem that a particular real input
was faithfully represented or that a received claim is true.

`invariant-path`'s accepted sortal ADR provides a useful distinct
third task: determining whether two presentations are the *same*
argument under specified identity criteria. Its ADR also explicitly
reports missing equivalence-certificate machinery. Do not call this
a completed triangulation theorem. A general construction of
types ↔ tropes ↔ sortals has not been located in the inspected
repositories, so the relationship remains a research question.

== Candidate proof obligations

[cols="1,3,4",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Claim to prove, with its assumptions |Needed implementation evidence
|X-01 |Lowering preserves the stated type judgement, and every runtime operation refines the typed effect/resource judgement |Typed source → IR → actual executor, negative controls
|X-02 |A type tag or schema equality cannot forge numerical subject identity, provenance or principal authority |Authenticated identity/sortal comparator, counterexamples
|X-03 |A declared trope transformation's IR grade soundly bounds its actual particularity loss |Per-construct faithful-lowering proofs and a declared observation model
|X-04 |If a sortal reports same identity under an allowed transition sequence, every permitted intermediate move preserves that sortal's invariant |Formal move relation and composition certificate or obstruction, not just hash equality
|X-05 |A position does not confer legitimacy on its occupant; authority is conditioned on independently valid occupancy evidence and interval |Institutional occupancy judgement + consumer enforcement
|X-06 |A proof receipt cannot upgrade mere claim/evidence to factive knowledge without an explicit sound-warrant premise |Bridge to epistemic-types' `SoundWarrant` / ProofTransport
|X-07 |A merge or split retains exactly its declared number and ancestry of particulars; honest fusion is distinguishable from conflation |Trope merge grades + provenance identity transition model + counterexamples
|X-08 |Combined typed and trope verdicts do not imply one another; each can fail while the other succeeds |Independent acceptance/rejection fixtures, and a formal independence proof where models allow
|X-09 |The same identity may be represented across multiple modality/role/time charts without illicit equality or a fabricated global clock |Chart-indexed witnesses, explicit partial maps, time and causal-order premises
|===

These are *open obligations*, not statements of proved results.
An inability to prove one under current assumptions should be
reported by its missing premise or countermodel.

== Possible experiments (not an agreed sequence)

The following order reflects low-risk experiments *if* VCL-UT adopts
this line of research. It should not be interpreted as committing
Firmboot, GNPL, Tangle, QuandleDB, KRL, Haec, Vocarium or Hermeneia
to VCL-UT's architecture, or as requiring an all-purpose identity
calculus before doing local correctness work.

=== Earlier draft: a staged programme

. **Vocabulary and discovery (now).** Publish an extensible,
  source-anchored, theorem-aware index of type disciplines and
  identity/particularity judgements; incorporate Mathesis and
  `nextgen-typing` rather than copying large cross-estate inventories.
  Record `PROVEN`, `TESTED`, `MODEL-ONLY`, `SPECIFIED`, `OPEN`
  separately. The old L0–L10 safety matrix remains intact.
. **Small proof bridge.** Propose a single carefully scoped
  identity–particularity use case: two schema-compatible records,
  distinct particulars, and an attempted MERGE. Test that static
  compatibility alone cannot license an identity conflation and that
  an explicit, appropriately warranted fusion can be represented.
  Do not assume all types/tropes have a common equivalence.
. **Source and runtime obligations.** Give the new checks explicit
  source forms only when their operational semantics, authority and
  evidence contracts are specified. Keep transition admission and
  commit permission separate; make the consumer fail closed.
. **Type-family graduation.** Add independent plugin/discipline
  boundaries and proof receipts for the families that genuinely add
  guarantees. When formal modelling requires a different prover,
  establish and review the bridge instead of labelling a heuristic
  detector a theorem.
. **Only then broader ontology.** Evaluate partial maps and
  context-relative identity across additional semantic, spatial,
  temporal, social, normative and evidential spaces. A claim
  crossing spaces must preserve exactly the invariants stated in its
  certificate, never a universal identity promised by default.

VCL-UT owns its language/admissibility design and proof obligations;
Mathesis inventories theorem evidence; Kategoria/TypeLL research and
implement type mechanisms; Haec/Vocarium/Hermeneia and trope-checker
own situated particularity, use and loss. Shared work requires reviewed,
versioned interfaces without changing ownership or collapsing
these independently meaningful objects.
