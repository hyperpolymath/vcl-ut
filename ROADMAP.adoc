The single most important qualification: *the L0–L10 predicates and
certificates are specified/proved over `Statement` values; VCL source reaches
at most L6.* `parse_statement` fixes `proof_clause`,
`effect_decl`, `version_const`, `linear_annot` and `epistemic_clause` at
`None`, and `infer_requested_level` awards L7–L10 on exactly those fields, so
the highest level reachable from source text is *L6 `CardinalitySafe`*. The
parser also fail-closes on modal expressions (`KNOWS`, `BELIEVES`,
`ANNOUNCE`, `COMMON`). The missing work is L7–L10 source grammar and
parse-to-AST elaboration/binding; ordinary schema binding of field references
already exists. Issue #25 concerned parser/corpus-boundary work, not K-CUT,
and is closed. See the
link:docs/audits/K-CUT-VCL-UT-RELATIONSHIP-2026-11-01.adoc[relationship and
source-gap audit] for the exact evidence and limits.

== Nearest Work

These are ordered by value, not by version number.

. *Surface syntax and elaboration for L7-L10.* Add parser support that
  constructs and binds `effect_decl`, `version_const`, `linear_annot`, and
  `epistemic_clause` (including the modal expression forms), then reconcile
  level inference across the source frontends. Until then, upper-level fields
  can only arrive through a constructed `Statement` or wire decode; they are
  not expressible in source. Issue #25 covered the earlier parser/boundary
  work and is closed; it did not establish a K-CUT dependency.
. *Execute the staged Agda Grade 3 port plan.* The current Idris2 corpus is
  engineering-proof evidence, not a Mathesis Grade 3 theorem receipt. Port the
  exact L0–L10 predicate/certificate statements in reviewable slices, with
  pinned Agda CI receipts and theorem-by-theorem assumption records. The plan
  is `verification/proofs/AGDA-GRADE3-PORT-PLAN.adoc`; no Grade 3 status is
  claimed until each in-scope theorem checks in Agda.
. *Wire `vclt-gate` into VeriSimDB.* Contract v1 is frozen and the producer
  works; the consumer (`rust-core/verisim-api/src/vcl.rs`) does not invoke it.
  Until it does, VCL-UT proves things about statements VeriSimDB never asks
  about.
. *Close the remaining L10 boundary gaps.* Proposition well-typedness of
  requirement expressions against the schema remains owed. Also align the
  Rust decider's direct reciprocal-cycle check with the Idris predicate's
  transitive-cycle check before relying on non-source (wire/programmatic) L10
  statements. The Idris transitive-cycle proof is already implemented; do not
  track it as outstanding.
. *Optional syntax cleanup: retire the legacy `HEXAD` spelling* when all
  supported frontends can do so compatibly. The current Rust parser accepts
  both `OCTAD` and `HEXAD` as the same `Source::Octad`; this is a source-name
  alias, not a six-modality profile. Its deprecation is independent of which
  modalities a deployment enables or which witnesses a subject populates.
  See link:docs/standards/MODALITY-PROFILES.adoc[the profile/Octad contract].

== Dependencies

* **TypeLL** type theory must stabilise for L6-L9. Note that L10 does *not*
  depend on TypeLL: `EpistemicSafe` originates here, in the shift from
  querying a store to addressing a consonance engine.
* **VeriSimDB** statement router must support VCL-UT as a third path alongside
  Slipstream (VCL) and VCL-DT.
* **ReScript evangeliser** must prove concept first — VCL-UT and AffineScript
  are the two showcase languages that follow.

== Future Directions

Once v1.0.0 ships:

* Federation support (VCL-UT statements across federated VeriSimDB instances)
* VCL-UT LSP language server for IDE integration
* Formal security audit of the injection-proof guarantee (L4)
* Academic publication — reframed around *consonance languages as a class*
  rather than a decalogue of query safety. The eleventh level is the reason:
  `EpistemicSafe` is not a query-safety property, and a paper titled after ten
  of them cannot house it.
